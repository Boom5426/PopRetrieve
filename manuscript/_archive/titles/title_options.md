# DART Manuscript — Title Options

Style constraints: honest, method-focused, no therapeutic-recommendation overclaim. Titles
should signal *conditionality* ("when", "conditions", "boundary", "diagnosing") so reviewers
see up front that failure regimes are characterized, not hidden.

## Ranked candidates

1. **Information conditions determine when distributional retrieval improves heterogeneous
   single-cell drug ranking** *(recommended — states the thesis and its boundary in one
   line)*
2. When does distributional retrieval help? A diagnostic framework for inverse drug ranking
   in heterogeneous single-cell populations
3. DART: information-conditioned distributional retrieval for heterogeneous single-cell
   drug ranking
4. Diagnosing when distributional information improves inverse drug retrieval in single-cell
   populations
5. Boundary-aware distributional retrieval for heterogeneous single-cell drug ranking
6. Mean-signature retrieval is a special case: a distributional view of inverse drug ranking
   and its information conditions
7. A benchmark and diagnostic gate for distribution-aware drug retrieval in heterogeneous
   cell populations
8. Distribution-aware inverse drug retrieval is gated by candidate-response information
   conditions
9. From mean signatures to distributions: when population structure improves single-cell
   drug ranking
10. Reliability-aware distributional retrieval for heterogeneous single-cell drug ranking

## Recommendation

**#1** for a methods journal (Cell Reports Methods / PLOS Comp Biol / Bioinformatics): it
names the mechanism (information conditions), the scope (heterogeneous single-cell drug
ranking), and the honest hedge (conditional advantage). **#2** if a question-form title is
preferred for a broader-audience venue. **#3/#6** if the method name "DART" or the
unification result should lead.

## Words to avoid in the title

- "therapeutic design", "precision medicine", "drug discovery"
- "predicts resistance", "predicts response"
- "universal", "outperforms", "superior", "optimal"
- "solves inverse design"
