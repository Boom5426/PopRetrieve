PopRetrieve / population-response retrieval

When do single-cell response distributions improve drug retrieval?

More cellular detail can improve drug ranking. PopRetrieve tests when it does.

Compare response direction, mean-response magnitude and full cell populations, then test what survives biological evaluation and prediction in a new cellular context.

Core principle

Higher cellular resolution matters when the added information changes the drug ranking.

01 / Framework

From response direction to a predicted cell population.

PopRetrieve treats representation, ordering, and prediction as separate empirical questions. The aim is to identify when a cellular response distribution supplies decision-relevant information beyond a well-specified mean baseline.

01 / Direction

Does a candidate align with the target response direction?

Direction-only cosine similarity compares mean response orientation while deliberately discarding response magnitude and cell-to-cell structure.

Direction is a useful starting point, but it cannot distinguish an advantage caused by response magnitude from one caused by population structure.

02 / Findings

A small observed gain. A harder prediction problem.

The paper separates what the mean already explains, what changes with the evaluation criterion, and what happens when candidate responses must be predicted.

01

The mean explains most of the gain

A magnitude-aware mean recovers most of the improvement over directional matching. With observed Tahoe responses, mean reciprocal rank is 0.945 for mean cosine, 0.965 for mean L2 and 0.976 for energy distance.

Figures 2 & 5
02

The evaluation changes the winner

The additional population benefit weakens under mechanism and functional evaluation. The same held-out protein measurements favour different RNA retrieval strategies when evaluated as means or full populations.

Figures 3 & 4
03

Prediction does not preserve the advantage

None of the six evaluated forward predictors shows a statistically supported population advantage. Generating state-dependent responses alone does not restore the gain measured with observed candidates.

04 / Software

Score the mean response and the cell population with one API.

Use population and mean-response metrics, prediction baselines, controlled benchmarks and experiment drivers from the Python package.

Compare representations on the same query.

Energy and MMD use the unbiased U-statistic by default. The installation and reproduction commands are maintained in the repository README.

    seed=7,
